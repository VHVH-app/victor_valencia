Questions to Evaluate:

  1. What was your first impression of this site?
  2. How does this site organize the flow of information? (Do they have links on the left and text on the right? Some other scheme? No scheme?)
  3. Does the site structure make sense or does it confuse you? Why?
  4. Does the site graphics/layout/multimedia enhance the site or detract from it? Why?
  5. How long did it take for the home page to load? Was the load time reasonable?
  6. Was the text color and size readable? If a background color or image was used, did it enhance or detract from the site?
  7. Did the site have a navigation bar or other navigation tool? Was the navigation well done?
  8. Did the site have a color scheme or look and feel that was repeated on all the pages?
  9. How does the site respond to smaller window sizes (responsive design)?
  10. What does this site offer that was unusual or very well done?

Pitchfork

www.pitchfork.com/

  1. At first I thought "oh great, a bunch of ads". This is because I saw the SUBSCRIBE button
  2. It starts from top to bottom, before reaching a point where there are two vertical lists side by side. Seems to be a mix of vertical and horizontal lists.
  3. It makes sense, but there is still too many articles on the page.
  4. I think it the graphics enchance it because the immediately draw your eyes in.
  5. The page loaded very quickly.
  6. Text and size are readable. News article headers are in big font, whicle sub-information is in smaller text. I also like that the main color is white. This makes it easy on the eyes.
  7. The site seems to have two navigation tools.
    • A navigation icon
    • A horizontal list of pages
  8. Yes, the standard black and white color theme was present in all pages.
  9. The site seems reactive, meaning that it conforms to the dimensions of the screen.
  10. I think the use of color was good. It is easy on the eyes to see empty white space. I don't like pages that try to cram every ince of space on a page.
